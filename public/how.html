How to use

Three tools. No account needed.

Video Downloader

Save a public video from a link.

  1. 1
    Copy the link

    In the app, tap Share, then Copy link.

  2. 2
    Paste it

    Tap Paste, then Get video.

  3. 3
    Download

    Pick a format and keep the page open until it starts.

QR Maker

Make a QR code anyone can scan.

  1. 1
    Pick a type

    Link or text, Wi-Fi, or WhatsApp.

  2. 2
    Type your details

    The code updates as you type.

  3. 3
    Save it

    PNG for sharing. SVG for printing.

PDF to Word

Turn a PDF into a Word file.

  1. 1
    Choose a PDF

    Tap the box, or drop a file on it. Up to 20 MB.

  2. 2
    Wait a moment

    It takes a few seconds to a minute. Keep the page open.

  3. 3
    Download

    After downloading, open the file and check the layout.

Questions

Why won't my link work?

Private videos, videos that need a login, and pages with several videos, like profiles or playlists, cannot be saved. Check that the link plays in your browser without signing in.

Why can't I convert my PDF?

Scanned PDFs are only pictures, so there is no text to edit. Password-protected PDFs need the password removed first.

Are my files saved?

No. Files are deleted right after you download them, or after a few minutes if you don't. QR codes are made on your device.

Can I add it to my home screen?

Yes. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. In Chrome, open the menu and choose Install app.
